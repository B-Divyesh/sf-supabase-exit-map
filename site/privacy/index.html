Privacy, kept local.

Supabase Exit Map is designed to avoid collecting project data. This notice explains the small amount of browser data involved in the optional paid feature.

The CLI

The CLI reads files beneath the directory you select. It has no analytics or telemetry and does not make network requests. Reports stay in your terminal or in the output file you choose. Do not publish a report before reviewing its file paths and evidence.

The website

This static website has no analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. Its service worker caches public site files on your device so the guide can work offline.

Local browser storage

If you choose a theme, restore a purchase, or save a Planning Room plan, the site stores that preference, license token, daily verification result, and plan in your browser’s local storage. Your imported report and planning choices are not uploaded. You can remove them by clearing site data in your browser.

Purchase and license verification

Buying opens Sociobot’s hosted checkout, with Dodo as merchant of record. Their checkout receives the purchase and payment information you provide under their policies. This site sends your license token to api.sociobot.in to verify it on first unlock and at most once per day afterward. The verifier may receive ordinary request metadata such as IP address and user agent. We do not send your project report with that request.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests, use the repository’s public issue tracker without posting credentials or private project data. Material changes will be dated on this page.
